- **Also:** Sparta II ablaze off Finland, EU–Indonesia trade pact, 128 states agree killer-robot rules by 2028, Bitcoin dives to $55,100, BepiColombo enters Mercury orbit, M6.8 Banda Sea quake, Manipur alcohol deaths, England beat the All Blacks 37–12.
- **Date check:** Kryvyi Rih, Russian missiles in Poland, Kyiv bridges hit, Cyclone Akvan's landfall, Virginia's measles death (20 Nov) and the Riyadh PIF Tower hit (19 Nov) are left out; the Las Vegas GP win, the ATP final (Alcaraz beats Zverev 6–2, 7–6(9–7)) and the COP31 no-deal close all happened Sun 22 Nov, after this day ended — every story here happened or was first reported on 21 November.

`main.py` copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` to the output directory (standard library only). `draw21.py` is the PIL script that drew the image from the verified 21 November stories.
